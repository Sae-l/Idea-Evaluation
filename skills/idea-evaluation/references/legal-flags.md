# Regulatory flags (jurisdiction-neutral; load only when relevant)
Not legal advice. Name only categories that apply, each as "verify with [authority/professional] in your jurisdiction". Ask for the user's country only if a flag would change the decision.

- **Employment / ownership:** side-work permission, employer IP claims on inventions or code.
- **Business formation and licensing:** trade registration, regulated trades and professions (electrical, gas, refrigeration, construction), insurance or liability cover.
- **Food, supplements, cosmetics:** registration, labeling, health-claim limits, safety assessment.
- **Electronics and products for sale:** safety marking (e.g. CE/UKCA/FCC), radio rules, battery and e-waste registration, product liability.
- **Medical or assistive devices:** may count as regulated medical devices.
- **Data and AI:** privacy law (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), AI-content labeling and platform rules, advertising disclosure, no unsupported health claims.
- **Finance and money handling:** payments, lending, investment advice and crowdfunding are often licensed.
- **Export and sanctions:** export controls and sanctions lists change; check current official sources.
- **Service at customer sites / heights / hazards:** liability insurance, safety training.
