# Inventions and hardware: extra checks (load only for technical ideas)

**Technology Readiness Level (TRL)**
1 principle observed · 2 concept formulated · 3 proof of concept · 4 lab prototype · 5 validated in relevant environment · 6 prototype demonstrated in relevant environment · 7 prototype in operational environment · 8 system complete and qualified · 9 proven in operation.
Report the current level and what the next level costs. Ideas at TRL 1–3 need cheap proof-of-concept tests before any business planning.

**Prior art / patent search (before disclosing publicly)**
Search free databases: Espacenet (EPO), Google Patents, WIPO PATENTSCOPE, plus national offices (e.g. USPTO, DPMA/DEPATISnet). Also search products, papers and open-source projects. Goal: is it new, and may I build it (freedom to operate)?
Caution: public disclosure (posts, demos, talks) can destroy patentability in many jurisdictions. For anything promising, consult a patent attorney or national patent information centre. This is not legal advice.

**Ownership**
Ideas from an employer's field, time, data or equipment may belong to the employer or a research institution. Check contracts and invention-disclosure rules before work starts.

**Cheapest build route**
Use existing open-source designs and off-the-shelf modules; simulate or prototype in software or cardboard first; contract manufacturing before own production.
